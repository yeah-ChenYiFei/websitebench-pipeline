we get it

we get it your pet means the world to you

Helping pets live longer and healthier lives means the world to us too. The American Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals® (ASPCA®) has been dedicated to protecting animals for over 155 years. That is why we formed a strategic cause partnership with the ASPCA in 2006.

see my rates

find the perfect fit

Pets and families are not one-size-fits-all

Customize your plan to get the price and coverage that’s right for you and your four-legged family member.

  • flexible coverage

    Add Preventive Coverage at a low additional to cost to help keep your pet healthy.

  • annual limit

    This is the amount you can be reimbursed over a 12-month policy period.

  • reimbursement rate

    we offer reimbursement percentage options of 90%, 80%, and 70%

  • Deductible

    Choose an annual deductible of $100, $250, or $500.

see my rates

we’re pet people too

Lots of our team members have pets as part of their families. They know firsthand how much joy our furballs bring to our lives. Our team also understands how stressful it is when a pet gets hurt or sick

You can count on our team’s support in times like these. They’re always ready to lend a hand and they truly care about the well-being of our customers’ pets.

why us?

Simple, we’re experts in pet insurance

Founded in 1997, we’re one of the oldest pet insurance companies in the US. Over the past 20 years, we’ve covered more than 600,000 pets while processing more than 2.2 million claims through our insurance underwriters.

With our experience and track record, you can rely on us to provide the coverage your pet deserves.

what makes us a leader of the pack?

It can be tough to choose between reputable pet insurance companies. But you’ll enjoy lots of treats with the ASPCA® Pet Health Insurance program.

treats we offer

Flexibility

  • Freedom to visit any licensed veterinarian or specialist

  • Preventive Care Coverage options at an additional cost

  • Easily manage your account with the ASPCA® Pet Health Insurance app.

Savings

  • 10% off when you enroll additional pets

  • Up to 90% cash back on covered veterinary care

  • 30 day money-back guarantee*
