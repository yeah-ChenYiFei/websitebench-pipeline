Our goal is to help you stay informed, prepared, and inspired as you explore new horizons. Let’s make every trip unforgettable, together.

Useful TripIt Features

At TripIt, we’re constantly looking for ways to make travel less stressful. These TripIt features can help make your next trip a breeze.

Nearby Places
Makes it easy to find places close to where you’re staying, including restaurants, ATMs, hospitals, and more.
Neighborhood Safety Scores
Shows safety scores for the areas you’re visiting, including overall safety, physical harm, women’s safety, LGBTQ safety, theft, and more.
Risk Alerts
Notifies you when a potential disruption, such as inclement weather, power outages, or protests, may impact your trip.
Fare Tracker
Notifies you if the price of your airfare drops after you book, and provides steps on how to request a refund or credit.
Documents
Allows you to upload PDFs, photos, boarding pass and mobile QR codes, and other useful documents to your travel plans.
Flight Alerts
Sends real-time flight alerts throughout your trip to give you a heads up about delays, cancellations, gate changes, and more.
