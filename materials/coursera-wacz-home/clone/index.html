Frequently asked questions

Is Coursera accredited, and are Coursera certificates recognized by employers?

Coursera partners with accredited universities and leading companies such as Google and IBM to offer courses, Specializations, and Professional Certificates.

Is a Coursera certificate worth it?

For many learners, a Coursera certificate provides job-ready skills and a verifiable credential from respected universities and companies.

What is Coursera Plus, and is it worth it?

Coursera Plus is an annual subscription that gives learners access to thousands of courses, Specializations, and Professional Certificates.

Does Coursera offer free online courses?

Coursera offers thousands of courses that you can preview for free, including access to the first module.

What are the most popular courses on Coursera?

Popular programs span AI, data science, cybersecurity, software engineering, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning.

How can Coursera help me get a job or advance my career?

Job-aligned Professional Certificates and Specializations help learners build employer-relevant skills.

What is Coursera for Business, and how much does it cost?

Coursera for Business helps organizations reskill and upskill employees. Pricing varies by team size and learning needs.
