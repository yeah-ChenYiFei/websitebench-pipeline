How to sell your plant machinery, fast

Take great photos

It may sound obvious - but taking good-quality photos can have a huge impact on the time it takes to sell your plant machinery. Adverts with good photos sell much faster.

Keep it snappy

Keep your machinery description short and simple. Call out optional extras that other similar plant machinery may not have. And don’t forget to mention full-service history if you have it.

Be honest

It's important that your description is accurate. If your machine has a small scratch on it – be honest about it. It'll save time with needless viewings if buyers are aware of any faults from the get-go.
