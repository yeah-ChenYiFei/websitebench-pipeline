How to sell your motorhome, fast

Take great photos

It may sound obvious - but taking good-quality photos can have a huge impact on the time it takes to sell your motorhome. Adverts with good photos sell much faster.

Keep it snappy

Keep your vehicle description short and simple. Call out optional extras that other similar motorhomes may not have. And don’t forget to mention full-service history and habitation checks if you have them.

Be honest

It's important that your vehicle description is accurate. If your motorhome has a small scratch on it – be honest about it. It'll save time with needless viewings if buyers are aware of any faults from the get-go.

Your questions answered

  • You could consider part-exchanging or selling your motorhome to a dealer, or selling your motorhome at auction. You could also use a motorhome broker, who work on commission and pay you once it's sold. You can also privately sell your motorhome online, which can be one of the quickest ways to sell your motorhome and get a good price for it.

  • While selling your motorhome to a dealer is one of the fastest ways, selling your motorhome online puts it in front of a large audience and can get you a better price. If you're selling your motorhome online, follow our tips on taking good photos for a strong advert, and find out how to prepare your motorhome for sale.

  • The value of your motorhome will depend on several factors including its age, condition and mileage. Unless you've bought a classic or significantly upgraded your motorhome, note that its value will likely have depreciated. To improve your chances of making a sale, compare your motorhome to similar classified ads and price according to the market.
