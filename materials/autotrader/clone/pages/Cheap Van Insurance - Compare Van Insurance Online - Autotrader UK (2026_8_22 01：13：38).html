Types of insurance cover

Comprehensive

Comprehensive insurance cover provides the most cover, protecting you, your van, and others in the event of an accident.

Third party, fire, and theft

Third party, fire and theft (TPFT) offers the same level of cover as third-party, but also covers you if your van is stolen or damaged by fire.

Third party only

The most basic van insurance policy; it only covers injuries or damage to other people. That means you won’t be protected if you’re injured, or your van is lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed by fire.

Your questions answered

  • What is private van insurance? Private van insurance is the type of insurance you take out if you plan to use your van for social, domestic, or pleasure reasons only.

  • It depends on the insurer, but you can get fleet van insurance for just two vans up to hundreds of vehicles.

  • Private van insurance is known as 'social use only'. Unless your van is strictly for personal use, you need business van insurance (also known as commercial van insurance). That applies even if you just use it for commuting to a single workplace.

  • Some insurers would accept no claims discount if you earned it on a van, car, or motorcycle, but it can normally only be used on one vehicle at a time.
