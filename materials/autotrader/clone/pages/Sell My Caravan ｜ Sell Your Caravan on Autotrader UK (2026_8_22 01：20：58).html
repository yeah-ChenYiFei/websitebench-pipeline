How to sell your caravan, fast

Take great photos

It may sound obvious - but taking good-quality photos can have a huge impact on the time it takes to sell your caravan. Adverts with good photos sell much faster.

Keep it snappy

Keep your vehicle description short and simple. Call out optional extras that other similar caravans may not have. And don’t forget to mention full-service history and habitation checks if you have them.

Be honest

It's important that your vehicle description is accurate. If your caravan has a small scratch on it – be honest about it. It'll save time with needless viewings if buyers are aware of any faults from the get-go.
