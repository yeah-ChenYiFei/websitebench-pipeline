100LayerCake

100 Layer Cake is a creative collaboration between friends and fellow designers Jillian Clark, Amanda Dawbarn, and Kristina Meltzer. Launched in 2009, our wedding blog and website celebrate, inspire, and facilitate the most special events in life - weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, engagements - all the things that make your heart fill with love. Our vendor guide, marketplace, DIY resource and inspiration gallery provide a unique resource to help people plan their events, big and small.

Jillian is our Real Weddings editorial manager. On an average day you can catch her combing through the hundreds of pretty submissions we receive daily. She also manages our editorial calendar and dreams up project ideas. If she wasn't blogging she'd be planning wedding or assisting her husband Scott Clark Photo.

Kristina is our Cakelet editorial manager (our family blog), and resident floral aficionado. She finds constant inspiration in the idea that you can put a lot of love and thought into even the smallest celebrations. When she's not working with her gals on 100 Layer Cake, she's probably scrolling through Pinterest or digging in the garden with her two boys.
