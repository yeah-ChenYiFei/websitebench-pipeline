Bring Creativity to Your Library

Creativebug promotes wellness and lifelong learning through the proven benefits of creativity. This easy-to-use, high-quality online art and craft resource offers unlimited access to thousands of classes for all skill levels—helping patrons and library staff reduce stress, improve mood, enhance mindfulness, and develop new skills. Now featuring Creativebug Borrow, with access to Gibson Guitar app and ArtistsNetwork

Professionally filmed & edited content taught by experienced art educators.

Not just a database. Creativebug is your library’s partner in craft programming.
  • Public performance rights for libraries to host in-person and/or virtual classes.
  • Support for in-library programming with free printables, materials lists and video tutorials.
  • One-on-one support with Creativebug’s library team to assist from set-up to success.
  • Live demos on how to use the Creativebug platform and admin portal for library staff.
  • Downloadable promotional materials provided by Creativebug to promote the platform, as well as new and seasonal classes.
  • Seasonal programming guides curated by our crafting experts with themed classes and projects.
We know you are busy. No need to be an art teacher, too.
  • Full access to the Creativebug website for patrons to learn in the comfort of their own home.
  • Hosted virtual live events for patrons to interact with artists.
  • Customer support for users from our team to give them the best experience possible.
Do we check all your boxes?
  • New classes from top artists released every week.
  • Downloadable patterns, templates, and recipes.
  • Personalized landing page for library systems.
  • Configuration with your API authentication servers.
  • Custom administrative portal with usage dashboard & personalized reports, promotional & training materials, and MARC formatted content for bibliographic record uploads.

With Creativebug Borrow, patrons can enjoy easy access to digital content from beloved creative brands, like the Gibson guitar app and Artists Network. One on-demand Borrow per platform can be “checked out” one week at a time through the Creativebug website, with no further integrations needed.

Creativebug Borrow is the perfect way for patrons to explore niche, creative interests through their local library.
