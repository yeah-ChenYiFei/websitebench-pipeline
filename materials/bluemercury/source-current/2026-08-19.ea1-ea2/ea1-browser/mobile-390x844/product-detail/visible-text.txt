Product Information
Description

The #1 dermatologist recommended vitamin C serum among medical aesthetic skincare brands, C E Ferulic serum is now proven to visibly reverse 10 years of aging signs* so you can age backwards. With an enhanced patent-pending formula including 2 new powerful ingredients to further support visible skin longevity, this daytime serum continues to deliver 8x stronger skin against environmental aggressors and is clinically demonstrated to reduce combined oxidative damage generated by UVA/UVB rays, ozone pollution, and diesel engine exhaust by up to 41%. Beyond environmental protection, this serum visibly improves 8 signs of aging, including the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, discoloration, loss of firmness, and brightness on all skin tones.

C E Ferulic vitamin C serum maintains its synergistic antioxidant combination of 15% pure vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid), 1% vitamin E (alpha tocopherol), and 0.5% ferulic acid to enhance protection against environmental damage caused by free radicals that can contribute to visible signs of atmospheric aging. This enhanced formula is now boosted with 0.3% Carnosine and 1.0% Endophytol to further promote visible skin longevity, provide additional antioxidant benefits, and fight inflammation.

*aging signs – lack of firmness and dullness.
