posting help

create a posting

Click create a posting in the header to start. The posting wizard walks you through the steps in order:

  1. Category — choose the category that best fits what you are posting (for example, housing, for sale, jobs, services).
  2. Location — choose the region where the posting should appear.
  3. Details — give the posting a title and a description, and set a price if one applies.
  4. Contact — choose how people can reach you (email, phone, or both) and provide the contact details.
  5. Photos — optionally add photos of the item, place, or service.
  6. Preview — review your posting exactly as it will appear to readers.
  7. Publish — confirm and publish the posting to the site.

Postings are free. There is no charge for creating, editing, renewing, or reposting in this offline clone.

edit a posting

You can edit your own postings from the my account page. Find the posting in your postings list and choose the edit option. Change the title, description, price, contact details, or photos, then save. Edits apply immediately to the live posting.

renew a posting

Renewing moves a posting back to the top of its category and search results. From the postings list on your my account page, choose the renew option for a posting. Renewal is available for active postings and can be done as often as the posting rules allow.

repost a posting

Reposting creates a new copy of an existing posting. This is useful when a posting has expired or been removed and you want to place it again. Review the copy before publishing, since reposting creates a new posting with its own identity and expiry.

delete a posting

To delete a posting, go to my account, find the posting in your postings list, and choose the delete option. Deleted postings are removed from the site and can no longer be found by search or viewed by readers. Deletion is final in this offline clone — there is no undo.

photos

You can add photos during creation or later by editing the posting. Photos are stored locally with this offline clone and are shown in search results, category listings, and on the posting page. Uploaded images should be clear and should show what the posting is about. You can remove photos or change their order when editing.

preview and publish

The preview step shows your posting the way readers will see it, including the title, price, description, contact method, and photos. Check that everything looks right before publishing. When you publish, the posting goes live immediately and appears in its category and in search results for its region.

posting rules

  • Post in the right category — a housing posting belongs in housing, an item for sale belongs in for sale, and so on. Miscategorized postings may be removed.
  • Post in the right location — choose the region where the posting is relevant. Do not post the same item in many regions.
  • No spam — do not post the same content repeatedly, and do not post advertising or off-topic material.
  • No scams — do not post fraudulent, deceptive, or misleading content, and do not ask for money transfers or payment before a deal.
  • Be honest — describe what you are offering accurately, including price and condition.
  • Do not publish other people's personal information in a posting.
