avoid scams & fraud

This page is the housing help topic, and it also carries the general safety guidance for anyone using this site — especially people searching for housing. Scams can appear anywhere on a classifieds site. The rules below will keep you safe whether you are looking for a place, a room, an item, a job, or a service.

deal locally, face to face

Meet the other person in person before any money changes hands. For housing, visit the actual unit and meet the landlord or current tenant. For items, meet in a public place during daylight hours. If someone refuses to meet, or always has an excuse for why they cannot show you the place or the item in person, treat it as a warning sign.

never wire money

Do not send money by wire transfer, money order, prepaid card, gift card, or any method that cannot be reversed. Scammers routinely ask for a deposit or "security fee" by wire before you have seen the place. A legitimate landlord shows you the property first and takes payment through an ordinary, documented channel.

If anyone asks you to wire money for a deposit, rent, shipping, or a "processing fee" — stop and walk away.

never share financial information

Do not give out your bank account number, credit card number, social security number, or other financial details to someone you met through a posting. A legitimate contact does not need your bank details to show you an apartment or sell you a used item. If a message asks for this information, it is a scam.

watch for common scam patterns

  • Too good to be true — a beautiful apartment far below market rent, or an expensive item at a tiny fraction of its value.
  • The owner is "abroad" — the poster claims to be out of the country and asks you to send money to "reserve" the place before you can see it.
  • Overpayment — someone "accidentally" sends too much money and asks you to wire the difference back.
  • Pressure and urgency — the poster pushes you to act immediately, before you can think or check anything.
  • Requests for personal details — asking for identification numbers or financial data "to run a credit check" before you have even met.

meet in person and verify

When you meet, verify that the person is who they say they are and that the place or item is what the posting described. Take your time, ask questions, and never let anyone rush you. If you are renting, see the lease or rental agreement before paying anything, and read it carefully.

report suspicious postings

If a posting looks like a scam, do not send money or personal information. Flag the posting so it can be reviewed, and contact us with the details. In this offline clone, reporting is part of the demo flow — flagging marks the posting for review locally.

summary

  • Deal locally, face to face.
  • Never wire money or use prepaid cards for a deposit.
  • Never share bank, card, or identity numbers.
  • Meet in person and verify before paying anything.
  • Flag and report anything that looks like a scam.
