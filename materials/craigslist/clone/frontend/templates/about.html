about craigslist

what this site is

This is an offline clone of craigslist, built as a benchmark fixture for evaluating website replication and diagnostic tooling. It runs entirely on your local machine: every page, style sheet, script, and data record is served from this repository, and nothing on this site connects to the internet. It is a demonstration of the craigslist experience, not the real craigslist.

Because it is an offline clone, no real classifieds are for sale or rent here. Postings, prices, locations, and users are local demo data created for evaluation purposes. No actual transactions take place.

the classifieds model

Craigslist works on a simple classifieds model. People create postings to advertise items, housing, jobs, services, gigs, and other things they want to sell, rent, offer, or find. Each posting belongs to a region and a category, and appears in search results and category listings. Interested people contact the poster directly through the contact method the poster chooses — usually an anonymous email relay or a phone number. The site itself does not broker the deal, hold money, or arrange meetings; buyers and sellers deal with each other directly.

This clone reproduces the core of that model: browse by region and category, search, read postings, reply, save favorites, save searches, and manage postings from an account.

community norms

The real craigslist relies on its users to keep the community working. The same norms apply here as guidance for how this clone is meant to be used:

  • Post in the right category and location so listings are easy to find.
  • Be honest in your postings and in your replies.
  • Respect other people's privacy and do not publish anyone's personal information.
  • No spam, scams, or deceptive postings of any kind.
  • Deal locally and in person when possible, and be careful with your money and your information.
  • Flag postings that violate the rules so the community can stay clean.

Since this is a local demo, abuse here affects nobody but the person running the fixture — but the behavior modeled by the site is meant to match the real community's expectations.
