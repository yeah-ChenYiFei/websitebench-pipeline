common questions

how do I post?

Use the create a posting link in the header. Choose a category and location, then fill in the details, add contact information and photos, preview your posting, and publish it. Full instructions are on the posting help page.

how do I search?

Type keywords into the search box on the home page, or pick a region and category from the home page and browse. You can refine results by category and location, and save a search to reuse it later from your account.

I can't access my account

Make sure you are entering the email address and password you registered with. If you forgot your password, use the forgot your password? link on the log in page. If you never received a verification code, remember that registration is limited to once every five minutes per email address, and that codes are delivered to the local offline outbox in this clone. See the account help page for details.

an action failed, what now?

Most failed actions show a short message explaining what went wrong — for example a missing field in a form, an invalid email address, or a posting that has already been removed. Read the message, correct the input, and try again. If an action still fails, note what you were doing and what message appeared, and contact us.
