privacy policy

offline clone · benchmark fixture · local data only

1. local data only

This site is an offline clone of craigslist that runs entirely on your local machine. Everything you do on it — browsing, searching, accounts, postings, saved searches, favorites, and messages — is stored locally in this repository's local storage. No data leaves your machine, and nothing is transmitted to any external server.

2. no tracking

The site does not use analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, or any other tracking technology. There are no tracking pixels, no embedded third-party content, and no remote scripts. The pages load only their own local styles and scripts from this repository.

3. no third-party cookies

No third-party cookies are used, and no third-party content is loaded from other domains. Because this is an offline demo, nothing on the page ever contacts another website.

4. session cookie note

The site uses a single local session cookie so you can stay signed in to your demo account while you use the site. This cookie is created and read only by this local clone, and it disappears when you close your browser session or log out. It is not shared with anyone.

5. data you enter

Data you enter — such as an email address for a demo account, posting text, or contact form messages — is stored locally and used only to exercise the clone's features. This is demo data; do not enter real sensitive information. Verification codes and recovery codes are delivered to the local offline outbox, not to a real inbox.

6. children

The site is a technical benchmark fixture intended for developers, testers, and evaluators. It is not directed at children, and no real personal information is collected from anyone.

7. contact

Questions about this privacy policy? See the contact page. For the rules of use, see the terms of use.
