account help

sign in

Use the my account link in the header to reach the log in page. Sign in with the email address and password you registered with. If you stay signed in, the header will show links to your account, settings, and log out.

If you enter the wrong password, the log in page shows an error — check the spelling and try again. Passwords are case-sensitive.

registration

Creating an account is free. From the log in page, choose the "create one" link to open the registration page. Enter an email address and choose a password, then confirm the password and agree to the terms of use.

After registration, a verification code is sent to your email address. In this offline clone, the code is delivered to the local offline outbox — no real email leaves your machine. Enter the code on the verification page to finish creating your account.

Registration is limited to once every five minutes per email address. If you try to register the same email address again too soon, you will see an error asking you to wait.

password recovery

If you forget your password, use the forgot your password? link on the log in page. Enter the email address you registered with and a recovery code will be sent to that address (again, delivered to the local offline outbox in this clone). Use the code to reset your password and then sign in with the new password.

managing your postings

From my account you can see every posting you have created and its status (for example active or removed). From the postings list you can:

  • edit a posting to change its details or photos,
  • renew a posting to move it back to the top of results,
  • repost a posting to create a new copy, or
  • delete a posting to remove it from the site.

See the posting help page for details on each of these actions.

saved searches

When you run a search you can save it from your account. Saved searches appear on the saved searches page, where you can run them again with one click or remove the ones you no longer need. Saving a search lets you return to the same set of results quickly without retyping your keywords and filters.

favorites

Postings you mark as favorites are kept on the saved postings page. Favoriting a posting does not notify the poster and does not reserve the item — it is just a private bookmark in your account. Remove a posting from favorites whenever you like.
