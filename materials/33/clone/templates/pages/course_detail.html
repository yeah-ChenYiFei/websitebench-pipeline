There are 4 modules in this course

In the first course of the Deep Learning Specialization, you will study the foundational concept of neural networks and deep learning.

By the end, you will be familiar with the significant technological trends driving the rise of deep learning and be able to build, train, and apply fully connected deep neural networks.

Introduction to Deep Learning

Week 1  •  2 hours to completeModule details

Explore the major trends that have driven the rise of deep learning and learn where neural networks work best.

Neural Networks Basics

Week 2  •  8 hours to completeModule details

Use a computational graph and vectorization to implement the foundations of a neural network.

Shallow Neural Networks

Week 3  •  6 hours to completeModule details

Build a neural network with one hidden layer and understand forward and backward propagation.

Deep Neural Networks

Week 4  •  9 hours to completeModule details

Build and train a deep neural network and apply it to computer vision.
