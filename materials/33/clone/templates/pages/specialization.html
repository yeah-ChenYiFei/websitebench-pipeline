Frequently asked questions

What is Deep Learning? Why is it relevant?

Deep learning is a branch of machine learning built around multi-layer neural networks.

What is the Deep Learning Specialization about?

This five-course series introduces the foundations and practical applications of deep learning.

What will I be able to do after completing the Deep Learning Specialization?

You will be able to build and evaluate neural-network systems for several common AI tasks.

Do I need to know programming or machine learning before starting?

You should be comfortable with Python and basic programming; a background in machine learning is helpful but not required.

What background knowledge is necessary to enroll?

Familiarity with Python and an intermediate level of math including calculus and linear algebra is recommended.

Do I need to take the courses in a specific order?

We strongly recommend taking the five courses in sequence, starting with Neural Networks and Deep Learning.

Will I earn university credit for completing the Specialization?

You can earn a shareable career certificate; eligible learners may apply coursework toward degree credit.

Can I enroll in just one course in the Specialization?

Yes, each course can be taken independently, and you can start with a free audit of any course.
