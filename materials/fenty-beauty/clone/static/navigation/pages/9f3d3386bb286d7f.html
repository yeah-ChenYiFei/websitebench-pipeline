STRAIGHT UP:

This game-changing waterproof foundation is engineered to flex, bend and stretch to every (e)motion without creasing or settling—no matter if you smile, cry or smooch. Its medium, buildable coverage delivers a natural matte finish that looks like skin and feels like nothing. Inspired by years of consumer notes, Rihanna took her original shade range back and started from scratch—fine-tuning undertones, creating more neutrals and testing all around the world. Now, more skin tones than ever can find their Fenty fit shade match in her best 50-shade range yet. Get started with our shade finder tool to get matched.
