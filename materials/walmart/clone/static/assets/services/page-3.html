FAQ

How does free battery installation with purchase work?

Free installation with purchase can apply at participating Auto Care Centers, but eligibility depends on both your selected battery and your vehicle’s design.

  • Confirm participation: Use the service page or call to check if your local center offers battery installation for your vehicle type.
  • Check eligibility: Verify that free installation applies to the battery you’re buying and that your vehicle allows standard under‑hood access.
  • Know exceptions: Some vehicles have battery locations that are more involved, which can change service terms.
  • Plan your visit: Bring vehicle details, and ask about walk‑in vs. appointment options.

Availability and terms can vary by location and vehicle configuration.

What affects the cost of battery installation?

Installation cost can vary based on where the battery is located and how your vehicle is designed.

  • Eligibility for free with purchase: Some installations may be free with a qualifying battery purchase if your vehicle allows standard access.
  • Vehicle design: Batteries located in less accessible areas (compared to typical under‑hood setups) can make service more involved.
  • Service approach: Store participation, coverage for your vehicle type, and local policies can influence fees.
  • Fit and components: Confirming terminals, hold‑downs, and orientation ahead of time can help avoid extra trips.

For the most accurate information, check with your selected Auto Care Center before visiting.

How do I choose the right battery size?

Start by matching what your vehicle requires, then narrow by climate and electrical needs.

  1. Check fit: Use your owner guidance and current battery label to confirm group size (e.g., Group 35, Group 24, Group 51R) and terminal position.
  2. Confirm hardware: Match hold‑down style and tray footprint for a secure install.
  3. Consider climate: If you drive in colder weather, compare Cold Cranking Amps (CCA) for stronger starting power.
  4. Pick battery type: Vehicles with start‑stop features or added electronics may benefit from AGM; many daily drivers use lead‑acid.

When in doubt, bring your vehicle details to an Auto Care Center for in‑store guidance.
