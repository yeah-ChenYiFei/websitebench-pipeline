FAQs

How much does this service cost and what's included?

Assessment: $0 with contracted insurance plans. Without insurance the assessment costs $50 if you are an established patient or $70 if you are a new patient.

Assessments are performed by our licensed and trained pharmacists. They include a blood pressure screening, a patient questionnaire covering relevant medical history information, and a conversation with our pharmacist to help determine which birth control options are recommended for you.

Prescription Contraceptives: May be available at no cost through insurance. Contact your insurance for coverage details.

Do you take insurance?

Coverage by insurance, HSA, FSA or HRA may vary. Contact your insurance for coverage details.

How can I be seen for this service?

Call your local pharmacy to make an appointment or walk into any of the Walmart Pharmacy locations in the states listed below & ask for the Hormonal Contraceptive Prescribing service.

Will I be able to get my prescription the same day?

Yes, prescriptions can be filled while you wait.

Will you have to contact my doctor for a prescription?

In most cases, the pharmacist will be able to prescribe without contacting a physician. Depending on the state & the pharmacist's assessment of your health conditions, a primary care provider may need to be consulted.

What does the assessment entail?

The assessment may vary by state & includes (but is not limited to) a series of questions related to your medical history, current contraceptive use, history of smoking & pregnancy screening, as well as a blood pressure test.

Where is this service available?

Currently, hormonal contraceptive prescribing services are available at all Walmart pharmacies in Arizona, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, Oregon, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, Washington, West Virginia, Wisconsin, & Wyoming.

What types of hormonal contraceptives are available?

Types of contraceptives vary by state. Where these services are available, contraceptive pills may be prescribed. In some states, alternative options, such as contraceptive rings, patches and injections, may be available to be prescribed. Check with your pharmacy for more information.

Are there any side effectives of hormonal contraceptives?

Side effects may vary & depend on the specific form of contraception used. Your pharmacist will discuss potential side effects when selecting which method of contraception is right for you.
