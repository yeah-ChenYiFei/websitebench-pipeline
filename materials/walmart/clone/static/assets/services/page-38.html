Medicare & health insurance enrollment

When is the Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (AEP)?

The Medicare Annual Enrollment Period runs annually from Oct. 15–Dec. 7.

What can I do during the AEP?

You can change, enroll in or add additional Medicare coverage.

Do I need to re-enroll in Medicare every year?

No, you don’t need to re-enroll every year. However, it’s important to review your plan’s most up-to-date information as benefits can change.

When will the changes made during the AEP take effect?

Changes made during the AEP will take effect on Jan. 1 of the following year.

What documents should I review during the AEP?

It’s important to review the Evidence of Coverage (EOC) & the Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) sent by your plan to ensure you understand any changes in your coverage.

What should I consider when reviewing Medicare plans?

When reviewing Medicare plans, it’s important to consider monthly premiums, benefits & whether the coverage still meets your healthcare needs.

When is the open enrollment period for major medical insurance?

In most states, the open enrollment period for major medical insurance is from Nov. 1–Jan. 15.

What are the requirements for enrolling in health insurance outside of the open enrollment period?

You can enroll in health insurance outside the standard open enrollment period if you qualify for a special enrollment period.

What are major medical insurance plans?

Major medical insurance plans, also known as marketplace plans, offer comprehensive benefits that meet the requirements of the Affordable Care Act (ACA).

How can I enroll in health insurance?

You can get major medical insurance from the federal marketplace, your state’s marketplace if it has one, through private insurance companies directly or through private marketplaces that represent different carriers.

You can also visit https://walmart.allstatehealth.com 24/7 to browse & compare local health insurance plans from nationwide carriers.

Or call 833-848-0061 (TTY 711), Monday through Friday, 8am to 8pm ET to speak with a licensed health insurance agent about your coverage options.

What should I focus on when choosing a health insurance plan?

When choosing a health insurance plan, focus on the benefits that are most important to you & ensure the plan meets your healthcare needs.

What type of assistance do agents in your stores provide?

In-store licensed agents can assist customers with both Medicare & major medical policies.

How can I find an agent’s schedule?

You can find an agent’s schedule posted on the insurance kiosk table or by asking the pharmacy staff.

Can agents help me choose a plan that suits my needs?

Yes, licensed agents can help you make sense of your options & pick a plan that best meets your needs.
