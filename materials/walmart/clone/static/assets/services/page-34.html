FAQ on Accounts & Eligibility

Does Walmart accept SNAP EBT, formerly known as food stamps?

Yes, Walmart accepts SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program) EBT cards formerly known as food stamps.

You can use your SNAP EBT card at all participating Walmart stores and online for pickup, delivery, or shipping.

Does Walmart accept both EBT SNAP & EBT Cash for online transactions?

Yes, Walmart accepts both SNAP EBT & EBT Cash for online transactions in all states for Shipping, Pickup, or Delivery where available.

Who qualifies for SNAP EBT?

SNAP EBT provides nutrition benefits to help families stretch their food budget. Eligibility is determined by your state. Participants must apply in the state they live in & meet certain requirements to qualify. Learn more about SNAP EBT eligibility on the USDA SNAP website:

https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program

Do I need to have a Walmart account to use SNAP EBT for online orders?

Yes, you need a Walmart account to place an online order for pickup, delivery or shipping. Learn more about ordering online here:

https://www.walmart.com/cp/1987240

Can I use SNAP EBT on the Walmart shopping app?

Yes, you can pay with your SNAP EBT card when shopping on the Walmart app. Add your SNAP EBT card to your account wallet before or during checkout, then enter your PIN to pay.

How do I sign up to use my SNAP EBT benefits online at Walmart?

Create an account on the Walmart app or site & add your SNAP EBT card info to your account wallet or enter it at checkout. You can search or explore the site for eligible items marked as “SNAP EBT eligible.” Once you’re ready to check out, add or select your SNAP EBT card, check your balance or adjust your payment amount as needed then check out securely with your SNAP EBT PIN.

Do I need a Walmart+ membership to use my SNAP EBT card on Walmart?

No, a Walmart+ membership is not required to use your SNAP EBT benefits. Walmart+ membership has savings & benefits, such as unlimited home delivery with no delivery fees, & free shipping. Customers who qualify for SNAP, WIC, Medicaid & more may qualify for a 50% discount on membership fees. Learn more here: https://www.walmart.com/plus/assist

Can I use WIC online?

WIC is accepted as an online payment method only in Washington state and Massachusetts. In all other states, WIC benefits are not supported for online orders and can only be used at participating Walmart store locations.

FAQ on Shopping & Checkout

Can I use my SNAP EBT benefits to place an online order?

Yes. SNAP EBT benefits can be used online for eligible items with Pickup, Delivery, or Shipping.

Learn more: https://www.walmart.com/cp/fulfillment-options/1987240

What items are eligible for purchase with SNAP EBT funds?

Eligible items are marked with the “SNAP eligible” badge on the Walmart App and site.

SNAP is a federal program administered by the USDA. Eligible food items are defined by federal SNAP EBT guidelines and your state. Walmart provides tools to help identify eligible items.

For the most accurate information, always consult with your state’s official SNAP EBT resources or call the phone number at the back of your card.

How can I tell which items are SNAP EBT-eligible on the Walmart app or website?

Look for the "SNAP eligible" badge on the item page or use the SNAP EBT eligible foods filter when you search on Walmart or visit the item page. You will also be able to see SNAP eligible items in your cart during check out. Walmart automatically applies eligibility rules based on the state where your order is being picked up or delivered

For the most accurate information, always consult with your state’s official SNAP EBT resources or call the phone number at the back of your card.

What items are not covered by SNAP EBT?

While most food items are eligible, some states may have added additional eligibility rules. Walmart automatically applies eligibility rules based on the state where your order is being picked up or delivered

For general USDA guidelines, visit: https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/eligible-food-items.

For the most accurate information, always consult with your state’s official SNAP EBT resources or call the phone number at the back of your card.

Only items purchased in store or marked as ‘sold and shipped by Walmart.com’ are eligible to be paid for by SNAP EBT cards. Marketplace items are not eligible for SNAP EBT benefits.

How do I check my SNAP EBT/EBT Cash balance?

You can check your balance at checkout. To check your balance at checkout, edit the payment amounts & cards at Review Payment. You’ll be prompted to enter your PIN before viewing balance. Customers can always call the number on the back of their card to check their EBT balance.

Can I buy non-SNAP EBT-eligible items with an alternative form of payment?

Yes, you can buy non-SNAP EBT-eligible items in the same order as SNAP EBT-eligible items. At checkout, you will be able to pay with your SNAP EBT card & another form of payment. Please note, only one SNAP EBT card can be added & used per order.

Why are some grocery food items not eligible to be paid with SNAP EBT?

Only USDA approved items are eligible. Some states may have restrictions on additional items. Only items bought in store or marked as ‘sold & shipped by Walmart.com’ are eligible to be paid with SNAP EBT cards. SNAP EBT benefits cannot be used to buy items sold by Marketplace sellers.

For the most accurate information, always consult with your state’s official SNAP EBT resources or call the phone number at the back of your card.

How do I check out with my SNAP EBT/EBT Cash card?

If your SNAP EBT card is already added to your account wallet & you have eligible items in your basket, you will see the card listed as a payment method on the Review Order screen. Otherwise, select "Edit payment" at Review Order & "Add a new payment method" to add your SNAP EBT card.

Can I make an edit after placing my order with SNAP EBT/EBT Cash?

Yes, you can make changes to a pickup or delivery order when it is still in the processing stage. Changes include adding or removing items or item quantities, editing substitution preferences, rescheduling your timeslot or store location or updating delivery address. In the event of a delivery address or pickup location change, SNAP EBT item eligibility may be impacted to meet state item eligibility rules.

Can I pay delivery fees using SNAP EBT funds?

No, delivery fees & other service charges may not be paid for with SNAP EBT benefits. Only eligible food items may be purchased with SNAP EBT benefits. You may pay for fees using your EBT cash benefits or another payment type.

What if I don't want to use all my SNAP EBT benefits or don't have enough funds to cover my order balance?

At checkout, you can adjust how much of your SNAP EBT balance to apply. If your balance doesn’t cover the order, add another payment type to complete your purchase.

You have the option to split your payment between a SNAP EBT card & another form of payment.

What is Weight Debit?

Some items, such as fruit and meat sold by weight, are estimated at checkout. A 10% weight debit surcharge may be added. If the final weight costs less, the difference will be refunded back to the card.

How do I get free shipping or delivery?

Customers can get free shipping by signing up for a Walmart+ membership. Walmart+ Membership has savings & benefits, such as Free same-day Grocery Delivery, unlimited scheduled home delivery with no delivery fees, & free unscheduled shipping. Customers who qualify for SNAP, WIC, Medicaid & more may qualify for a 50% discount on membership fees. Learn more here: https://www.walmart.com/plus/assist

FAQ on Substitutions & Refunds

What if my items go out of stock?

If an item has been paid using SNAP EBT but is not available, any substitutions you allow for will be charged in the following manner:

• If you do not select a backup method of payment for the order but allow for substitutions, we will substitute with an item that is the same or lower priced as the original item.

• If you select a backup method of payment for the order and we must substitute with an item of higher price, any difference in cost for your substitution will be charged to the alternate payment card. Amounts charged to non-SNAP EBT payment methods will be subject to tax.

Can I return food items purchased with SNAP EBT?

Yes, for SNAP EBT orders with a receipt, an item can be returned and funds refunded back to the SNAP EBT card or exchanged for another unit of the same item. Return of funds by cash, credit or gift card is not allowed. See additional details on our return policy here:

https://corporate.walmart.com/policies#return-policy
