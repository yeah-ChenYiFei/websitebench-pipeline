FAQ

How do I send money once I’m at a Walmart store?

When you arrive at your local Walmart store, visit the Customer Service Desk or Money Center. Be sure to bring your photo identification. You can pay with either U.S. currency or a PIN-based debit card. Use the store locator to find the nearest store.

What do I need to initiate the transfer?

You need to provide the sender’s name, address, acceptable photo ID, date of birth & phone number. You may also need to provide information about the recipient.

What photo identification is accepted?

Photo identification is needed when sending or receiving money. A valid government-issued photo identification that is not damaged or expired is an acceptable form of ID. This includes:

• U.S. driver’s license, including Puerto Rico

• State ID, including Puerto Rico

• United States passport

• International passport

• Military ID

• U.S. permanent resident card

• Tribal ID

• Mexican matricula consular

Can I send money to a bank account or mobile wallet?

Yes, options include cash, bank account, debit card, mobile wallet & home delivery. Availability varies depending on the service provider & the country of destination.¹

Where can money be received?

To receive a money transfer, visit the Customer Service Desk or Money Center & present a valid photo ID. You can also pick up at MoneyGram & Western Union agencies.

When will the money be available for the recipient?

With a few exceptions, cash will be ready to be paid out in as little as 10 minutes, except for transactions that need further review by Western Union, Ria or MoneyGram.²

Who do I call if I have an issue?

For Ria transactions via Walmart2Walmart or Walmart2World, call Ria’s customer line at 1-855-355-2144.

For MoneyGram domestic or international transactions including Walmart2World, call MoneyGram at 1-800-926-9400.

For Western Union domestic or international transactions including Walmart2World, call Western Union at 1-844-982-4287.

How do I track my transfer?

Visit the provider’s website & track your transfer using the details on your receipt.
