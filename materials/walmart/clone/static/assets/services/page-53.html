Spark Good Frequently Asked Questions

What is Walmart Spark Good?

Spark Good brings all our local giving programs under one brand and empowers nonprofits to engage with customers and associates to support causes they care about. Walmart customers can round up their online purchases to verified nonprofits and NCES schools with Spark Good accounts or donate needed products to these organizations.
