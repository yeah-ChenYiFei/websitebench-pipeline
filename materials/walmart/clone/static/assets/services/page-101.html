Tax-Exemption FAQ

I’m already tax-exempt with a Walmart store. Do I have to apply for tax-exempt status again?

Currently, the store and online application process are separate. You will need to enroll your Walmart Business account online in the Walmart Tax Exemption Program.

Go to https://business.walmart.com/account/taxexempt to review the steps and apply.

I’m already tax-exempt on Walmart.com. Do I have to apply for tax-exempt status again?

If you have signed up using the same email on Walmart.com & Walmart Business & if you have approved tax-exempt status on Walmart.com, then you do not need to apply for tax exempt again. After creating an account on Business.Walmart.com (Walmart Business), your tax-exempt status should be available in the Walmart Business account.

If your email information is different on Walmart.com & Walmart Business then at this time you will need to submit an online enrollment by navigating to Business.Walmart.com/Taxexempt

If I enroll online, will my tax-exempt status transfer to Walmart Stores?

Yes, if you complete an online tax-exempt application on Business.Walmart.com/Taxexempt and are approved, then your tax-exempt status extends to stores when using Walmart Pay with the Walmart Business App.

If you do not use Walmart Pay, then kindly refer to the instructions in the following FAQ.

How do I shop tax-exempt in store?

If you are enrolled in Walmart’s Tax Exemption Program through Walmart Business, you can apply your tax-exempt status to purchases in store using Walmart Pay in the Walmart Business app or you can use your tax-exempt ID number:

Manned/Staffed Register:

-Checkout using Walmart Pay in the Walmart Business app as your payment method, or

-Show your tax-exempt ID to the Walmart associate before paying. You can present it in the Walmart Business app or as a printout (make sure it has the Walmart Business header).

Self-Checkout:

-Using Walmart Pay: Scan all your items & pay using Walmart Pay. Taxes will be automatically removed from eligible items.

-Using Other Payment Methods: After scanning your first item, tap the 3 dots in the top right corner while on the home screen, select "Tax Exempt," enter your tax-exempt ID number, confirm your organization name & proceed with payment.

Where can I find my Walmart tax-exempt ID to shop in-store?

You can find your Walmart tax-exempt ID number in a few ways:

In the Walmart Business app: Once you are logged in, go to the “Account” page & select "Tax exemption" to see your digital card with your Tax-Exempt ID. If you have the most recent version of the app then the option to find the Tax-Exempt ID should be present on the in-store mode pop up screen when one is in the store.

From a mobile browser or desktop: Users who are logged in can go to “Account”, then select “Tax exemption” to see your digital card with your Tax-Exempt ID.

Can I edit my approved enrollment?

If your organization is already approved for tax exemption, you have two options:

a. Deactivate Your Enrollment: The primary admin can deactivate your tax-exempt status if you are closing your business or no longer want tax exemption for your organization.

b. Add a State to Your Enrollment: You can add additional states to your existing tax-exempt enrollment. Please note: Your tax-exempt status will be temporarily disabled until you receive an approval email for the additional state(s).

Can I edit my deactivated enrollment?

No, if your enrollment is deactivated, you will need to begin a new enrollment. Your previous enrollment submission is no longer eligible for resubmission.

What expiration date do I use for my enrollment?

The online enrollment is set to automatically default to a three-year expiration date. If your state-issued tax-exempt documentation includes an expiration date, please use the date shown on your certificate.

How do I renew an expired enrollment?

You will need to start a new enrollment under your existing account as your prior submission contains outdated information. Starting a new enrollment will temporarily disable tax-exempt status until you have resubmitted & received an approval email.

I have an approved enrollment, why was I charged tax on my purchase?

Tax exemption applies only to state(s) you are approved for, qualifying items, and permitted quantities.

There are a couple of reasons why you might see taxes on your purchase:

a) Only certain items are tax-exempt. Items within restricted categories such as Firearms, Alcohol, or Tobacco items may not be eligible for tax exemption.

b) You shipped to a state where you don’t have tax-exempt status. If you need to ship outside of your state, choose the states on your application. Upload a license for each state.

If you need help regarding tax charges, please reach out to taxexempt@walmart.com

Is there a limit to how many items I can purchase tax-exempt?

When using Walmart Pay at self-checkout, there is no limitation to how many items you can purchase tax-exempt.

When checking out through an manned/staffed register, there is a limit. Please check with the associate first if you have a large quantity purchase.

FAQ

How can business buyers ensure tax-exempt eligibility for bulk janitorial supplies?

To ensure tax-exempt eligibility, business buyers must provide a valid tax-exempt certificate during account setup or checkout. This allows businesses to purchase janitorial supplies without sales tax. Walmart Business simplifies the process by verifying tax-exempt status for qualified buyers.

What types of food service products are available for tax-exempt purchases on Walmart Business?

Walmart Business offers tax-exempt eligibility for various food service products such as bulk utensils, disposable containers, and cleaning solutions. These items are ideal for businesses like restaurants and catering services. Ensure your tax-exempt status is verified for streamlined purchasing.

Why should businesses consider tax-exempt purchases for office technology solutions?

Tax-exempt purchases for office technology allow businesses to save on essential items like printers, monitors, and networking devices. This is especially beneficial for bulk buyers setting up or upgrading office spaces. Walmart Business provides tax-exempt options for eligible accounts.

Which facilities maintenance products are tax-exempt eligible for bulk buyers?

Facilities maintenance products, including cleaning supplies, safety equipment, and repair tools, may qualify for tax-exempt purchasing through Walmart Business. Business buyers can reduce costs on essential products while ensuring compliance with tax exemption requirements.

Where can I find delivery options for tax-exempt orders on Walmart Business?

Tax-exempt orders on Walmart Business can be placed online for delivery to commercial locations across the United States. Ensure your account is verified for tax exemption before ordering. Walmart Business offers shipping to facilities, offices, and other commercial categories, ensuring convenience and access for janitorial, food service, and technology supplies.
