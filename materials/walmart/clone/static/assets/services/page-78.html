What are the check-cashing limits & fees?

In most states the check-cashing limit is $5,000, though we increase this limit to $7,500 between January & April of each year.

Check-cashing fees have a $4 maximum for pre-printed checks up to $1,000. For pre-printed checks above $1,000, the maximum fee is $8. Two-party personal checks are limited to $200 & have a maximum fee of $6.

How do I cash a check?

Simply present your endorsed check to the cashier at our Money Service Center or Customer Service desk along with valid identification & pay the required fee. There’s no need to register to cash checks with us or to have a Walmart store credit card or Walmart MasterCard.

How can I skip a trip to the bank with in-store check cashing at Walmart?

We make it easy to cash your check while shopping. You’ll get your money immediately & save time doing it. Plus, there’s no registration required. Want to load cash on a Walmart MoneyCard for extra convenience? Easily get your card online & pay no issuance fee.
