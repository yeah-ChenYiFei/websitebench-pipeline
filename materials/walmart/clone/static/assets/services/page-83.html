FAQ

What do Wireless Sales Specialists do in Walmart stores?

Wireless Sales Specialists are dedicated professionals who are here to enhance your shopping experience. They help with wireless activations for both postpaid & prepaid plans, suggest wireless accessories & wearables, as well as offer care plans & insurance to meet your needs.

How can Wireless Sales Specialists assist me in Walmart stores?

Wireless Sales Specialists are ready to engage with you in the electronics department. They can activate your postpaid & prepaid phone & can help you choose & attach wireless accessories.

What kind of support do Wireless Sales Specialists provide for customers who have purchased wireless products?

They provide in-store professional support for customers who have purchased wireless products & services from Walmart.

How do Wireless Sales Specialists help me understand my chosen wireless services?

Wireless Sales Specialists educate & consult customers to ensure customers understand the wireless products & services that best suit their needs.

Can Wireless Sales Specialists demonstrate and explain features of wireless products?

Yes, they offer to demo, support & explain features of all wireless products sold to customers who request such assistance.

When are Wireless Sales Specialists available in Walmart stores?

Customers can find Wireless Sales Specialists in the electronics department in select stores during the following hours: Sunday to Thursday: 11am to 7pm Friday & Saturday: 10am to 8pm
