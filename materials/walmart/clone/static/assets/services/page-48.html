Baby Registry FAQs

How do I redeem the free house cleaning?

Free cleaning for one bedroom, one bathroom, kitchen, and common areas in a single home ($99 value) from our trusted third-party partner.

How to redeem:

1. Maintain your registry for at least 7 days.

2. Add 20 or more unique items to your registry.

3. Spend $300 on items from your registry (this can be you or someone you’ve invited)

How do I become eligible for a Baby Registry Welcome Box?

Earn your Baby Registry Welcome Box in a few simple steps:

1. Create your baby registry. Registries created 2/1/2025 & onward are eligible.

2. Registry must be open for 7 days.

3. A minimum of 20 unique items added to your registry. Registry needs 20 individual items, not a quantity totally 20. i.e.) 20 different unique items, not 20 boxes of diapers.

4. Over $25 purchased from and through your registry at Walmart. Items need to be purchased through your unique registry link to qualify. Items marked as “purchased” do not count towards total.

Once I have met the eligibility requirements, how do I claim my Baby Registry Welcome Box?

Once you’ve reached eligibility, a “Redeem” button will appear under the “My Registry” tab. Once selected, agree to the Baby Registry Welcome Box Terms & claim your box. Baby Registry Welcome Box offer is valid in the U.S., excluding PO Boxes.

What items are included in the Baby Registry Welcome Box?

How do I add items to my registry while I’m in the store?

1. Open your Walmart app & click “My Items” to locate your registry.

2. Once you are in your registry, select “Scan item to add".

3. Scan barcode of any in-store item to add your registry/list.

How do I know a gift has been purchased?

1. Once the gift has been shipped, we’ll send you the tracking details. We won’t reveal the gift or how much it cost.

2. After the item has been delivered, we’ll share the gift message & who purchased it for you. We’ll also include a gift receipt for returns*.

*Certain items, including marketplace items, are not available to return in-store. Please see our returns policy for details: https://www.walmart.com/cp/returns/1231920?povid=registryCP_customHTML_RegistryFAQs_returnpolicy

I purchased the gift from another retailer, how do I change the item to purchased on the registry?

While in their registry, the registrant can change an item’s “Purchased” & “Need” quantity by selecting “Manage” under each registry item.

1. Changing the “Need” & “Purchased” quantity to be equal will update the item’s status to Purchased

2. Purchased - Purchased: 2 & Need: 2

3. 1 of 2 Purchased — Purchased: 1 & Need: 2 Only registrants can manually adjust the “Purchased” & “Needed” quantity

* Items marked as “purchased” do not count towards total for Baby Box.

I am purchasing items in-store, how do I checkout so my gift is marked as purchased on the registry?

On the registry page, select “Print” to view all the registry items & barcodes for checkout.

1. Find the gifts in-store & head to the checkout counter.

2. Let the cashier know you are making a purchase for a registry. Show the cashier the registry barcode from your printed registry page or from your Walmart app.

* Items marked as “purchased” do not count towards total for Baby Box.

FAQ

Why should I make a baby registry?

Creating a baby registry helps you organize everything you’ll need for your new arrival, making it easier for friends and family to choose gifts you’ll actually use. With a registry, you can:

  • Track essentials like diapers, bottles, and nursery items in one place
  • Share a single list with guests, reducing duplicate gifts
  • Access tools to manage must-haves versus nice-to-haves
  • Qualify for perks like a free baby welcome box with sample items

It’s a practical way to stay organized and make sure you’re prepared for each stage of early baby prep.

How does Walmart’s baby registry work?

Walmart’s baby registry lets you create a personalized list of baby essentials, which you can easily share with family and friends. Here’s how it works:

  • Sign up and start building your registry online
  • Add items across categories like feeding, diapering, and nursery gear
  • Organize your list by priority and stage
  • Share your registry link for group gifting or baby showers
  • Track purchases and manage updates in one spot

Walmart also offers a free baby welcome box when you meet eligibility requirements, so you can try out sample-size basics before making bigger decisions.

What’s inside a free baby welcome box?

A free baby welcome box usually includes sample-size essentials to help you discover what works best for your routine. Common items you might find are:

  • Diapers and wipes for newborn care
  • Bottles and pacifiers to test feeding options
  • Baby skincare samples for bath time

These samples give you a chance to try different formats before adding full-size products to your registry. Contents can vary, so it’s a good idea to check the latest details on the registry page before qualifying.
