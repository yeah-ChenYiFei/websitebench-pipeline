Frequently asked questions

What is Walmart Pharmacy Delivery?

Walmart Pharmacy Delivery is a convenient service that delivers your delivery-eligible prescription medications right to your doorstep, with or without all your other groceries and essentials, making it easier than ever to stay on top of your health needs.

How does pharmacy delivery work?

Once your prescriptions are ready at the pharmacy, you’ll receive a notification* on how to get started. Or you can sign into your pharmacy page at Walmart.com/pharmacy.

From your pharmacy page, add your delivery-eligible prescriptions to your cart, and when ready, you can checkout as you normally would. During checkout, select when and where you'd like your prescriptions delivered. Once the driver has picked up your order, you’ll get a notification* that your prescriptions are on the way.

*Notifications will be received through email (if you have a connected pharmacy account), app push notifications (if you have them turned on), and text messages (if you have signed up for pharmacy texts).

Who is eligible to have prescriptions delivered?

Most patients are eligible. Eligibility criteria may vary based on prescription type, insurance limitations, and delivery area. Contact the pharmacy if you have questions about your eligibility.

Are there fees associated with the delivery service?

Delivery fees vary based on the delivery type selected (Express, 3-hour or Standard). Walmart+ members waive the standard $9.95 delivery fee, express and 3-hour fees still apply.

Additionally, when a prescription is in the cart, there are no minimum basket fees.

How do I sign up for pharmacy delivery?

First, you'll need a Walmart account and a connected Walmart Pharmacy account. For more information on how to create an account, visit the Create or edit an account and Add a pharmacy account pages.

Second, you'll need to enable prescription delivery, which can be found in the Pharmacy Account Information section of your pharmacy account at Walmart.com/pharmacy. Note that the pharmacy account information section is only available if you have successfully connected your Walmart.com to Pharmacy. (see above)

Can I use my insurance for prescriptions delivered?

Yes, we accept most major insurance plans for prescriptions.

What types of medications can be delivered?

We can deliver most types of medications, including refrigerated, reconstituted, maintenance medications, specialty medications & over-the-counter products. Some exceptions may apply.

How are controlled substances handled during the delivery process?

Controlled substances are not eligible for delivery at this time.

What other items can be delivered with my prescription?

A wide variety of items can be included with your prescription delivery, including groceries, electronics, household essentials & more. Use search to find items & filter by how you want to receive them. Look for availability information on the item page & search results page. Delivery-eligible items will be delivered along with your prescription.

Ordering & delivery process

How do I place an order for pharmacy delivery?

Once your prescriptions are ready at the pharmacy, you'll receive a notification* that includes instructions on how to have eligible prescriptions delivered.

You can also sign in to your account at Walmart.com/pharmacy to start the process.

*Notifications will be received through email (if you have a connected pharmacy account), app push notifications (if you have them turned on), and text messages (if you have signed up for pharmacy texts).

How long does it take to receive my delivery?

Delivery times vary but typically range from same day to a few days, depending on your location & available option.

What are the delivery hours?

Delivery hours are generally the same as pharmacy business hours, but specific times may vary. Check your local Walmart Pharmacy to confirm.

Is same day prescription delivery available?

Same day prescription delivery is available, depending on availability.

Can I have my family's prescriptions delivered along with my own?

Yes, to have your family members' prescriptions delivered along with your own, you must first link that individual to your pharmacy account. Go to 'My account section' then select 'Manage family members' and add your family members.

Do all my family members have to be W+ enrolled to schedule pharmacy delivery?

No, not all family members need to be enrolled in W+ to schedule pharmacy delivery. But the individual looking to schedule delivery must be W+ enrolled and must have the family member already added to their account in order to have their prescriptions delivered. Go to 'My account section' then select 'Manage family members' and add your family members to add them to your account.

Can I schedule a specific delivery time?

Yes, you can often choose a preferred delivery window when placing your order.

How will I know when my prescription is out for delivery?

You'll receive notifications via the mobile app, text message or email when your prescription is out for delivery.

What happens if I’m not at home when my prescription is delivered?

Customers can choose unattended or attended delivery. With unattended delivery, your order will be placed at your doorstep & you’ll receive an email update with a picture of the delivery. With attended delivery, someone must be there to receive & sign for the order.

Can I track my delivery?

Yes, most deliveries can be tracked in real time on our website or mobile app.

What if there’s a delay in receiving my order?

Our goal is to get your order to you as quickly as possible. Refer to our delivery help center for more information. https://www.walmart.com/help/article/pickup-and-delivery/d0d02a5f54e54592930f110aaf6a2f50

Payment & insurance

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept credit & debit cards, plus Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA) or Health Savings Accounts (HSA) in certain cases.

Can I use my Flexible Spending Account (FSA) or Health Savings Account (HSA) for payment?

Yes, FSA & HSA cards are accepted for eligible purchases.

How do I update my insurance information?

You can update your insurance details through our website, mobile app or by contacting the pharmacy.

Prescription management

How do I transfer my prescriptions to a Walmart Pharmacy?

From the Walmart pharmacy page at Walmart.com/pharmacy select Transfer prescriptions, provide us with which prescriptions you wish to transfer and your existing pharmacy details, and we will handle the rest.

Can I set up automatic refills?

Yes, many prescriptions are eligible for auto-refill. To enable auto refill for your prescriptions, please contact the pharmacy. However, please note that auto refills cannot be automatically scheduled for delivery at this time.

Once your auto refilled prescriptions are ready at the pharmacy, you will receive a notification* on how to schedule delivery.

*Notifications will be received through email (if you have a connected pharmacy account), app push notifications (if you have them turned on), and text messages (if you have signed up for pharmacy texts).

How do I update or change my delivery address?

You can update your address online, through our mobile app or by calling customer support at 1-800-Walmart.

What should I do if my prescription needs a prior authorization?

Our pharmacy team will work with your doctor & insurance company to obtain the necessary authorization.

How do I cancel or change an order?

You can edit or request cancellation for most items & orders before shipment by logging into your Walmart account & finding the order in your purchase history. For details on your edit or cancellation window, eligibility & options, refer to the Edit or cancel an order page on walmart.com/help.

Safety & privacy

How is my personal & medical information protected?

We adhere to strict privacy & security standards to protect your personal & medical information. You can find more details about Walmart's privacy & security practices on corporate.walmart.com/privacy-security/walmart-privacy-notice

What safety measures are in place for delivering medications?

Medications are delivered in tamper-evident packaging & stored at appropriate temperatures during transit.

What should I do if my delivery is lost or stolen?

Contact customer support or the pharmacy immediately for assistance.

How are temperature-sensitive medications handled during delivery?

  • Insulated Packaging: Refrigerated Pharmacy Delivery medications are placed in insulated bags that maintain safe and regulated temperatures, ensuring medications arrive in optimal condition.
  • Light Exposure Protection: The bags used for packaging are constructed with light-blocking materials that shield sensitive prescriptions from UV and ambient light exposure.
  • In-Person Delivery: Customers can schedule the delivery around their availability and must sign for their delivery via electronic signature.
  • Tracking Delivery: Customers can track their order status in real time through the Walmart app.

Special circumstances

Can I get a delivery if I’m out of town or on vacation?

Yes, we can arrange for delivery to an alternate address if needed, as long as the prescription is eligible for delivery and filled at a pharmacy within the delivery range of the alternate address.

What if I need an urgent or emergency prescription?

Contact our pharmacy for urgent or emergency prescriptions.

How are deliveries handled during extreme weather conditions?

We prioritize the safety of your medication & our delivery personnel. Deliveries may be rescheduled if necessary.

What should I do if I have questions about my medication?

Contact our pharmacists directly through our customer support channels for any medication-related questions.

Customer support

How can I contact customer support?

Visit our Help Center or call 1-800-Walmart, via email at Help@Walmart.com or through our website’s chat feature available at Walmart.com/Pharmacy/Help.

What are the customer support hours?

Our support team is available during regular business hours. Specific times can be found on our website.

Can I speak to a pharmacist if I have questions about my medication?

Yes, our pharmacists are available to answer any questions you may have.

How do I provide feedback about the delivery service?

You can provide feedback through our website, app or by contacting customer support.
