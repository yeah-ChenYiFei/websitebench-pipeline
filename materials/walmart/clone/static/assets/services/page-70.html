OnePay Credit FAQ

Will seeing if I pre-qualify impact my credit score?

You can see if you pre-qualify with no risk to your credit score.

Where can I use my OnePay CashRewards Card?

You can use your OnePay CashRewards Card anywhere Mastercard is accepted, as soon as you are approved.

How do I earn my welcome bonus?

You’ll earn your welcome bonus when you open a OnePay Card. The welcome bonus is awarded as points, which you can redeem as cash back into a OnePay Cash account or as a statement credit.

What is the OnePay Walmart Spend Card?

The OnePay Walmart Spend Card is a credit card that can only be used at Walmart and Walmart.com. When you apply for a OnePay Card, you will first be considered for the OnePay Cash Rewards Card; if you do not qualify for a OnePay CashRewards Card, you will be considered for a OnePay Walmart Spend Card. You may not apply for the Walmart Spend Card directly.

What is Walmart+?

Walmart+ is Walmart's membership program helping members save more time & money with exclusive benefits. With Walmart+, you get free delivery on groceries & more with $35 minimum order, gas savings, video streaming + so much more!
