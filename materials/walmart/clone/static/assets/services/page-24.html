      Frequently Asked Questions

      Have more questions? Find all answers here.

      To change your password, sign into your Walmart Contacts account. Navigate to "My Account" and select "Profile", where you'll find the option to update your password. Enter your current password and then your new password to make the change.

      To order contacts simply follow these steps:

      • Find your contact lenses by searching for your brand, manufacturer, or lens type.
      • Select your quantity of lenses – the more you buy the more you save – then proceed to the checkout process.
      • Enter your prescription details or upload a copy of your Rx if you have one. If you don't have your Rx handy, include your doctor's information and we can handle the verification directly with your eye doctor.
      • Complete the checkout process and choose between FREE standard shipping or expedited shipping for faster delivery.
      • Enjoy the rest of your day with peace of mind that your contacts will arrive at your door, right on schedule.

      Yes, a valid contact lens prescription from a licensed eye care professional is required to place an order. If you don't have a copy handy, provide your eye doctor's name or practice, and we'll verify your prescription directly with them.

      Most orders ship within 1-2 business days after prescription verification. Once shipped, delivery usually takes 5-7 business days. You'll get a tracking number so you can follow your package.

      We accept major credit and debit cards, including Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express.
