Registry FAQs

How do I add items to my online registry?

It’s easy! Just find an item you want & select the “Add to registry” button that appears on its description page.

How do I know a gift has been purchased?

As the registrant, you can check which gifts have been purchased for you on your registry page.

1. Once the gift has been shipped, we’ll send you the tracking details. We won’t reveal the gift or how much it cost.

2. After the item has been delivered, we’ll share the gift message & who purchased it for you. We’ll also include a gift receipt for returns*.

*Certain items, including marketplace items, are not available to return in-store.

Please see our returns policy for details: https://www.walmart.com/cp/returns/1231920

I purchased the gift from another retailer, how do I change the item to purchased on the registry?

While in their registry, the registrant can change an item’s “Purchased” & “Need” quantity by selecting “Manage” under each registry item.

· Changing the “Need” & “Purchased” quantity to be equal will update the item’s status to Purchased

· Purchased - Purchased: 2 & Need: 2

· 1 of 2 Purchased — Purchased: 1 & Need: 2 Only registrants can manually adjust the “Purchased” & “Needed” quantity.

I am purchasing items in-store, how do I checkout so my gift is marked as purchased on the registry?

On the registry page, select “Print” to view all the registry items & barcodes for checkout.

1. Find the gifts in-store & head to the checkout counter.

2. Let the cashier know you are making a purchase for a registry. Show the cashier the registry barcode from your printed registry page or from your Walmart app.
