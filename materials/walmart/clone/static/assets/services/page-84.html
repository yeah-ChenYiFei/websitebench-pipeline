System Selection & Replacement

Which Bryant equipment is right for my home?

System size and specifications depend on several factors, including:

• Square footage

• Insulation

• Window placement and configuration

• Geographic location

• Ductwork size and layout

Your local Bryant dealer will perform an in-home load analysis to recommend the system that best fits your home and your family’s comfort needs.

Should I repair or replace my heating and cooling system?

Every home is different. During your consultation, your Bryant dealer will inspect your system, review maintenance needs, discuss costs and consider your heating, cooling and air quality priorities.

Bryant systems and accessories are built with a commitment to quality and reliability. Whether you choose repair or replacement, Bryant dealers will do Whatever It Takes® to complete the job properly.

How do I know if I need to replace my HVAC system?

Signs include frequent repairs, uneven temperatures, rising heating and cooling bills, or if your system is over 10-15 years old.

Does this program offer solutions for indoor air quality?

Yes. Bryant offers air purifiers, humidifiers, dehumidifiers, and other indoor air quality products to help improve the air you breathe.

Service, Maintenance & Support

Can I schedule maintenance or repairs for my existing system?

Yes. Bryant dealers offer preventative maintenance services and can troubleshoot or repair equipment from most manufacturers.

How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?

It is recommended to schedule biannual tune-ups – once in the spring for cooling and once in the fall for heating – to help keep your system running efficiently.

Warranty & Coverage

How long is Bryant’s warranty coverage?

Warranty coverage begins at installation. Length of coverage depends on:

• Product type

• Warranty registration status

• Original purchaser vs. subsequent owner

• Installation setting (single-family, multi-family, or commercial)

Your Bryant dealer can provide detailed warranty information during your consultation.

What does Bryant’s limited parts warranty cover?

Bryant warrants functional parts against defects in materials or workmanship under normal use and maintenance.

Covered functional parts may include:

• Blower motor

• Compressor

• Coil

• Control board

• Heat exchanger

Labor is not included under the limited parts warranty. Contact your dealer or installer for full coverage details.

Is labor covered under the Bryant warranty?

Bryant provides a limited parts warranty as the manufacturer. Labor coverage, if offered, is determined by your dealer or contractor.

Please consult your installing dealer for specific labor warranty terms.

What parts are not covered under warranty?

Non-functional parts are not covered. These may include:

• Belts

• Pulleys

• Brackets

• Fuses

• Gaskets

• Paint

• Refrigerant

• Screws, nuts and bolts

For complete details, contact your dealer or installer.
