Business printing FAQs

To place a print order, you'll need a Business Printing account. If you already have a Walmart Business account, use the same login details.

To create a new account:

  • Go to Account and choose Business Printing Account

  • Enter your business email and create a password

  • Provide your organization’s name, EIN, phone number, and address

  • Submit your information to complete registration

Only digital sign-ups are accepted—phone or mail sign-ups are not available.

If you already have a Business Printing or Walmart Business account:

  • Visit My Account

  • Enter your email and password

  • Click Sign In

You'll be directed to your dashboard, where you can place and manage print orders.

If you need assistance, Customer Care is available to support you with any account, order, or verification questions.

  • Log in and visit the Help section in your dashboard

  • Or, access Customer Care for direct support options

Walmart Business Print is designed for business customers who need printing for professional use.

  • Use Walmart Business Print if you're ordering on behalf of a company or require a business account (with EIN)

  • Use Walmart Photo if you're ordering for personal or non-business needs

If you're unsure, Walmart Photo is best for one-off or personal prints, while Walmart Business Print offers a curated assortment of customizable business products & business designs, from education to non profit to healthcare & more.

New organizations are verified during account creation for security purposes.

  • Primary Admins will receive an email to verify the business

  • Buyers/Admins will be asked to contact their Primary Admin

  • Once verified, a green checkmark appears next to your organization’s name in My Account

Incorrect details may delay or prevent verification, so double-check all information before submitting.
