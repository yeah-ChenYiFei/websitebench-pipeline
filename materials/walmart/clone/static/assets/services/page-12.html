$4 Prescriptions at Walmart Pharmacy

Is my medication available for $4 prescriptions at Walmart?

Walmart Pharmacy offers some of the most common generic drugs prescribed at commonly prescribed dosages for many different conditions. If you take medications for cholesterol, diabetes, high blood pressure, mental health issues or thyroid problems, check the drop-down list above to see if your medication is listed. Terms & conditions apply.

How much will my prescription cost at Walmart?

If your medication is on the list, 30-day supplies start at just $4 & 90-day supplies or more start at $10. Prices for some drugs may vary & might be higher in some states, including California & Minnesota. Check out the program details or call your local Walmart Pharmacy for details.

Does Walmart $4 prescriptions include antibiotics?

No, antibiotics, antihistamines & steroids are not included in the $4 Prescription Program. See the program details or call your local Walmart Pharmacy to see if your medication is on the $4 prescription list.

Does Walmart Pharmacy do 90-day prescriptions?

Yes, you can get a 90-day supply of common generic medications starting at just $10. Prices & availability may vary by location. Call your local Walmart Pharmacy to see if your prescription is available in a 90-day dose. Terms & conditions apply.

How do I transfer my prescription to Walmart Pharmacy?

Check out our Walmart Pharmacy page to learn how to transfer your prescription to Walmart Pharmacy quickly & easily. Just click "Transfer prescriptions" to set up your account online.
