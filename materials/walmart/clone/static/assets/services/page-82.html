Assembly & Installation FAQs

What is Angi?

Angi is your home for everything home. A go-to for home projects big & small, Angi connects you with highly rated home service professionals, & ensure fair & transparent pricing for everything from house cleaning to major remodels. When you book an Angi service through Walmart, you’re covered by Angi’s Happiness Guarantee up to the full purchase price plus limited additional damage protection.

Who will complete my service?

Your service will be performed by a fully equipped professional on the Angi platform, who will arrive & complete the service backed by the Angi Happiness Guarantee. Angi vets all professionals who perform services for Walmart customers to ensure that they are connecting you with qualified local providers.

Who can I talk to about my service appointment?

Reach out for help anytime. Angi’s customer support team is standing by to assist you every step of the way, 24/7. You can email at Walmart@angi.com or give them a call at (877) 600-3455.

How can I reach my professional?

You will be able to communicate with your professional once your booking has been created & claimed by a local provider. You’ll be able to call or chat with the provider prior to the booking up until completion. Angi will send you the details on how to connect with your pro via email.

Is the service quality guaranteed?

When you book with Angi you’re covered by the Angi Happiness Guarantee. Angi will cover your service up to the full purchase price & provide limited damage protection. If you’re not satisfied with your service, Angi will work to make it right.

How do I reschedule my service appointment?

You will have several options for rescheduling your service appointment through Angi: online, via the app or by calling Angi’s customer support team directly. Angi strongly encourages Walmart customers to reschedule directly with their professional. However, you can always email Angi at Walmart@angi.com or give them a call at (877) 600-3455.

How do I cancel my service appointment?

You can cancel the service up until the day prior to your scheduled appointment date online, via the app, or by contacting Angi’s support team at Walmart@angi.com. Or give Angi a call at (877) 600-3455.

Do I need to reschedule my service appointment if my Walmart order is canceled?

No, your service appointment will automatically cancel as well.

What terms & conditions apply to my Angi booking?

Services that you book & pay for in full on Walmart’s website are governed by Angi’s Book Now terms & conditions. For more complex services for which you request a quote from Angi, Angi will provide you with a proposal that will include terms specific to your project.

Additional questions

If you still have additional questions, please give Angi a call at (877) 600-3455 or email Angi at Walmart@angi.com
