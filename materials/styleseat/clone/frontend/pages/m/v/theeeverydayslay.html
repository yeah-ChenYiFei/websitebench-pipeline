About Me

StyleSeat Verified
Hey there! I’ve been a hairstylist for 19 years and counting, and my passion for hair is as strong as ever. I stay up-to-date on the latest treatments, styles, trends, and techniques to ensure your hair thrives. Your happiness is my happiness, and I absolutely love what I do! I specialize in hair growth and retention, and I’m known for having “growing hands.” Healthy hair is my top priority, so I offer complimentary shampoos with most services to ensure clean hair for a slayed style. Whether you have natural or processed hair, I’ve got you covered. As a Master Stylist, I’m familiar with all hair types and textures from every ethnic background. With 17 years of mastering my craft, I’m proficient in various techniques like interlocking, palm rolling, and locksmithing, and I’m always eager to learn more. I offer seamless lock repairs, replacements, and extensions. I’m also an extension specialist, skilled in K-tips, micro links, nano links, sew-ins, tape-ins, and a variety of lock extension styles. As an Army vet, I’m well-versed in AR 670-1 regulations. So, my Battle Babes, you’ll be surprised at the looks you can rock while you serve. We believe in confidence without compromise, so get ready to be slayed! Take a moment to check out my detailed service list. I’m confident in my abilities, and I want you to feel just as confident when you sit in my chair and when you leave the salon. Be sure to call or text the number on file, Styleseat messages are not always relayed or received. I look forward to seeing you and making every day a slayed day! YES I HAVE HAIR ON HAND!! Deposit Policy

Vicky, A Healthy Hair Advocate requires a deposit to be paid at time of booking in order to secure an appointment. This deposit is non-refundable, and will be kept if you cancel without rescheduling. If you have any questions about your deposit, please contact Vicky, A Healthy Hair Advocate.

No-Show / Late Cancellation Policy

If you cancel less than 24 hours prior or fail to show up at your appointment, your deposit will be kept and you will be charged the difference between your deposit and 50% of service price for cancelling with less than 24 hours notice and 100% if you fail to show up at your appointment.
