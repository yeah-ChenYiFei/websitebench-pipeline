About Me

Top Pro
StyleSeat Verified
Professional Braider | Knotless, Box & Protective Braids | McKinney, and its environments. With over 20 years of hands-on experience, I am a professional braider specializing in knotless braids, box braids, cornrows, and protective styles for women and children. I focus on clean parts, neat finishes, and long-lasting braid styles that maintain healthy hair. From classic cornrows and intricate braid patterns to medium and small knotless braids, Bora Bora braids, and custom protective styles, each service is tailored to your hair needs and lifestyle. As a cosmetology graduate, I take pride in creating styles that not only enhance natural beauty but also protect and promote healthy hair growth. I work with children, teens, and adults, offering patience, precision, and a professional experience at every appointment. I proudly serve clients in Celina, Prosper, McKinney, and surrounding areas. Many of my clients are repeat and referral bookings, and new clients are always welcome. Please note: No shampoo service. Kindly review service descriptions before booking.

Deposit Policy

Beauty by D' Princess requires a deposit to be paid at time of booking in order to secure an appointment. This deposit is non-refundable, and will be kept if you cancel without rescheduling. If you have any questions about your deposit, please contact Beauty by D' Princess.

No-Show / Late Cancellation Policy

If you cancel less than 24 hours prior or fail to show up at your appointment, your deposit will be kept and you will be charged the difference between your deposit and 50% of service price for cancelling with less than 24 hours notice and 100% if you fail to show up at your appointment.
