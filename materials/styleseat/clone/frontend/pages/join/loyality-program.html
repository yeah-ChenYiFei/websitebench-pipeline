How do clients know about my Loyalty Program?

Your clients will automatically see your Loyalty Program details when they book with you. StyleSeat also highlights it in appointment reminders and your booking page to encourage repeat visits. Is the program automatic?

Yes. Once it’s set up, StyleSeat tracks visits, applies rewards, and notifies clients automatically — no manual work required.

What if I don't want to offer discounts all the time?

You’re in control. You can pause, edit, or turn off your Loyalty Program whenever you want, depending on your business goals or season.

Do clients have to sign up separately?

No. Clients are automatically enrolled once they book with you — making it effortless for them and easy for you to reward loyalty.
