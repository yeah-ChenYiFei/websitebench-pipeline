FAQ
What payment methods does the Reader accept?

The StyleSeat Reader accepts all major debit and credit cards, plus contactless payments like Apple Pay and Google Pay. Your clients can pay however they prefer — quickly and securely.

How do I set up my Reader?

Setup is simple. Connect your Reader via Bluetooth in the StyleSeat app, follow the on-screen instructions, and you’ll be ready to take payments in minutes.

What are the transaction fees?

Enjoy our competitive fees as low as 2.2%–2.5% + $0.30 per transaction with no hidden fees.

Can I get paid instantly?

Yes. When you use the Reader, your earnings are eligible for Instant Deposits, so you can access funds right after completing an appointment.

Is the Reader required to take payments?

No. You can still process payments directly through the app without a Reader. The Reader is just an easy, hands-on option for contactless or in-person checkout.
