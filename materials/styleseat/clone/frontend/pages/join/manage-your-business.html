Optimize Your Schedule

  • Power booking - Double book appointments on services with client downtime to make the most of your time.

  • Add gap time - Create quiet spaces in your schedule to prepare or rest between appointments.

  • Waitlist - Fill last-minute openings with a waitlist to keep clients satisfied.

  • Prebooking - Reschedule repeat bookings with reminders to prebook at the end of the appointment and recurring appointments for returning clients.

Customize Your Calendar

  • Color code to help you organize and visually understand at a glance.

  • Simply drag and drop to change your bookings and automatically alert clients of changes.

  • Control future appointment scheduling by selecting how in advance clients can book you or send automatic monthly availability alerts for clients to book.

  • Set your regular working hours or add vacation time so your schedule is always up to date for clients.
