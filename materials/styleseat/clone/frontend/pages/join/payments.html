FAQ
What are StyleSeat's processing fees?

StyleSeat’s processing fees are competitive and transparent — no hidden costs. You’ll pay a small percentage per transaction, and every payment is automatically tracked in your dashboard for easy reporting and tax prep.

Do I need a card reader?

No card reader needed. Clients can pay directly through StyleSeat using their saved card or mobile wallet. It’s fast, secure, and contactless — perfect for busy professionals.

Can I get paid instantly?

Yes. With Instant Deposits, you can access your earnings right after an appointment instead of waiting days for standard bank transfers.

Can I handle walk-ins or retail sales?

Absolutely. You can manually add walk-ins or retail purchases to any transaction and process payments directly in StyleSeat, keeping all your earnings in one place.
