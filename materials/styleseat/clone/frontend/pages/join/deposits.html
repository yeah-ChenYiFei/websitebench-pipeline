FAQ
Can clients get refunds?

Yes. You control when refunds are issued. If a client cancels within your policy window, you can choose to refund the deposit right from your dashboard.

Can I set different deposit amounts?

You can customize deposit amounts by service — whether it’s a flat rate or a percentage of the total cost — giving you flexibility based on the type or length of appointment.

How do deposits help reduce no-shows?

Deposits secure your clients’ commitment by requiring payment upfront. Clients are more likely to show up or reschedule responsibly when their appointment is backed by a deposit.
