FAQs

Can I customize the questions on my intake forms?

Absolutely! With StyleSeat as a salon management software, our salon client intake form builder allows you to have full control. Choose from multiple question types to get the info you need. Multiple Choice gathers specific answers quickly. Free Response lets clients share their service history or other details. Photo Upload asks for pics of their current look or dream style. Policy or Terms shares important policies before the appointment. Signature collects digital signatures for waivers and consent forms.

How do I make sure my client fills out the form?

After booking an appointment, clients will find the salon client intake form in the "Appointments" section of their account. Once they fill it out, you'll see the completed form in the appointment details. While creating your form, you can choose to make specific questions mandatory for clients to answer.

Why should I sell products in my salon, spa, and barbershop?

Selling products at your salon not only helps clients maintain their salon look at home but also boosts your revenue. With StyleSeat, adding products at checkout is seamless, making it easy to manage everything through your salon inventory management software.
