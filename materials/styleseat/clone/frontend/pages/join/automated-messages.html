FAQ
What types of messages does StyleSeat send automatically?

StyleSeat sends appointment confirmations, reminders, rebooking prompts, and thank-you messages — all timed perfectly to keep your clients engaged and reduce no-shows.

Can I turn certain messages off?

Yes. You’re in full control. You can choose which messages to send and adjust timing based on what works best for your business.

Are messages sent by text or email?

Both. StyleSeat automatically sends texts and emails depending on your client’s communication preferences — so they never miss a message.

How is this different from other salon reminder tools?

Unlike basic reminder systems, StyleSeat’s automations are fully integrated with your calendar and client history. Messages go out automatically at the right times — no extra apps, manual work, or missed clients.
