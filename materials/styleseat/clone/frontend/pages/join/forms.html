Can clients complete forms before their appointment?

Yes. Clients can fill out digital intake or consent forms ahead of time, so you have all the information you need before they arrive.

Absolutely. You can tailor forms to fit your services — adding questions, checkboxes, and fields specific to your business.

Can clients upload photos?

Yes. Clients can attach reference or inspiration photos directly to their forms, helping you prepare for their appointment in advance.
