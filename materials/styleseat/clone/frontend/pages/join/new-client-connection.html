When StyleSeat brings you a brand-new client, a one-time New Client Connection fee applies after the service is completed. There are no upfront costs — you only pay when you get paid.

Yes. You can view all your New Client Connection bookings, fees, and total earnings directly from your dashboard to track your ROI and see your growth in real time.
