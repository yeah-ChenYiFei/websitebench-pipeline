FAQ
How does Smart Pricing work?

Smart Pricing automatically adjusts your prices based on demand, time, and appointment type — helping you earn more during peak hours while keeping your schedule full during slower times.

Can I control which clients get Smart Pricing?

Yes. You decide who sees Smart Pricing. Returning clients can keep your standard rates, while new or high-demand appointments can be adjusted automatically.

Is Smart Pricing automatic?

Once enabled, Smart Pricing runs on its own — no extra work for you. You’ll still have full visibility into all price changes before they’re applied.

Will clients see changing prices?

Clients will always see their final price before booking. Smart Pricing updates happen behind the scenes, so your pricing stays transparent and professional.

Is Smart Pricing included in my plan?

Yes. Smart Pricing is included in the StyleSeat Premium Plan — helping you maximize every booking without any manual adjustments.
