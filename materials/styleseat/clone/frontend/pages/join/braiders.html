FAQ

Real questions from real braiders

Can I price by size and length?

Yes. Build each style with its own sizes, lengths, durations, and add-ons: small knotless at waist length books at a different price and time block than large at shoulder. Clients pick exactly what they want, so you never type out another quote.

How do deposits and no-show protection work?

You set the deposit, a percentage or a flat amount, and clients pay it to lock the slot. No deposit, no booking. Late cancels and no-shows are charged automatically to the card on file, at the rates you set in your policy.

Do my clients have to stop booking through Instagram?

No. Instagram becomes the front door. Put your StyleSeat link in your bio; clients tap it, pick a style, pay the deposit, and land on your calendar without a single DM. Your page stays the portfolio, StyleSeat runs the books.

Can I make clients come prepped?

Yes. Attach prep rules and intake forms to any service: washed and blow-dried, no heavy products, inspo photo required. Your reminders repeat them automatically before every appointment.

What does StyleSeat cost?

You start with a free trial. After that it’s a simple monthly subscription with payment processing as low as 2.2%. See current plans at styleseat.com/join/pricing.

My book is already full. What’s in it for me?

Protection and time back: deposits on every slot, fees that collect themselves, AutoCheckout, and rebook nudges timed to takedowns. And when you do want new clients, they’re already on StyleSeat searching for braiders in your city.
