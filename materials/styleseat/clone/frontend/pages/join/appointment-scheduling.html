FAQ
How does the automated waitlist work?

The automated waitlist fills your schedule faster by offering open spots to clients on your list the moment a cancellation happens. StyleSeat automatically notifies them, so you can keep your calendar full without lifting a finger.

Can clients reschedule on their own?

Yes. Clients can easily reschedule appointments through their confirmation or reminder messages — no manual back-and-forth required. You’ll be notified instantly when a change is made.

How do recurring appointments work?

Recurring appointments let your regulars lock in their preferred times in advance. You can set the frequency, duration, and end date, so clients always have their spot — and you can plan your calendar with confidence.
