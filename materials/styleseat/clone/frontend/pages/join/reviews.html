Frequently asked questions

Is StyleSeat legit?

Yes. StyleSeat has operated since 2011, has processed over 200 million appointments, and is used by independent beauty professionals across the U.S. It is rated 4.9 on the App Store (288,000+ reviews) and 4.8 on Google Play (60,000+ reviews).

How much does StyleSeat cost for professionals?

StyleSeat charges a monthly subscription plus a one-time fee when the marketplace brings you a brand-new client; payment processing is a flat per-transaction rate. There are no contracts, and you can start with a free trial. Current numbers are on the pricing page.

Is StyleSeat worth it for independent stylists?

For most independents, yes — pros who rate it highly say the marketplace clients and no-show fees alone cover the subscription. It is the strongest fit if you want new-client discovery built in; if you only need a calendar for an existing clientele, compare flat-fee tools first.

Does StyleSeat take a cut of my earnings?

No ongoing commission. You keep your service revenue; StyleSeat charges the subscription, standard card-processing rates, and a one-time fee only on the first appointment a new marketplace client books with you.

Can I switch to StyleSeat from Booksy or another app?

Yes. You can import your client list, rebuild your service menu, and share your new booking link the same day. Most pros report completing the switch within a weekend.

How do I leave a review on StyleSeat?

Clients are invited to review after a completed, verified appointment — open the appointment in the app or the post-visit email and tap "Leave a review." Only clients who actually booked can review, which keeps ratings credible.

Where else can I read StyleSeat reviews?

Independent reviews live on the App Store, Google Play, Trustpilot, and Capterra. We link to them because third-party ratings are the fairest way to judge any platform — including ours.
