CATEGORY STANDARDS FOR SUCCESS

Effort and Preparation (40%) 

Student meets project benchmarks on time; project evidences effort and commitment

  • Student meets with TF to discuss project before the week 5 session and comes well-prepared
  • Student submits project proposal before the week 6 session
  • Project proposal is complete and evidences effort and self-review
  • Student workshops project with peers during week 9 and comes well-prepared
  • Project progresses between the week 5 meeting and week 9 workshop session
  • Writing and/or other product evidences effort and self-review
  • Shareable component evidences effort and self-review 
  • Student presents at the showcase and comes well-prepared

Topic Choice (10%) 

Project clearly addresses an ethical issue pertinent to the student’s experience(s)

  • Project clearly addresses and/or informs a normative ethical question
  • Project explicitly includes normative ethical or social reasoning or analysis
  • Student motivates the importance of the chosen issue or topic
  • Student explicitly connects the project topic to one or more of their own experiences

Integration of Course Material (25%) 

Project integrates concepts/tools covered in the course

  • Student explicitly incorporates at least one concept or tool discussed in the course (i.e., uses, discusses, critiques, or builds upon it)
  • In doing so, student demonstrates mastery of that concept or tool

Creativity and Rigor (25%) 

Project evidences independent thought and analytical rigor

  • Student demonstrates independent thought, including independent normative thought (i.e., student does more than report others’ ideas)
  • Student analyzes an ethical issue, breaking it into parts and employing critical reasoning skills
  • Student’s reasoning and analysis is rigorous
